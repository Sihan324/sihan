## Interpretation points to keep in mind

- The live-birth ARIMAX model uses TFR as a predictor — it is a conditional forecast, not a standalone projection.
- The model-trend TFR scenario should be labelled as a model-implied path, not a policy expectation.

- TFR and live births are associated, but the model does not establish causality.
